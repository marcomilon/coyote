# Ventas314 — Pricing decision (Oct 15, 2026)

## Final model
| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| 1st design | Free (1 per business, verified by WhatsApp; preview only until published) |
| Extra designs | 3 for USD 4.90 |
| Publishing (custom domain + hosting, all included) | **USD 100/year** (consider USD 99 for display) |

Pitch: "Tu primer diseño es gratis. Publicalo con tu dominio por USD 99 al año, todo incluido. ¿Querés más opciones? 3 diseños por USD 5."

## Unit economics (assumptions: USD 1 per AI design, ~USD 12/yr domain, ~USD 1/mo hosting, 5% payment fees)
- Margin per site: 100 − 24 − 5 = **USD 71/year (~USD 5.90/month)**
- Free design cost at 2% conversion: USD 50 per paying client → year-1 margin ≈ USD 21; year 2+ USD 71/year
- Extra design pack: USD 5 − 3.25 cost = USD 1.75 margin
- Fixed costs assumed USD 50/month

## Targets
- USD 2,000/month for Marco ≈ 350 sites at USD 100/year
- Realistic mix: direct clients + 3–5 agencies (wholesale USD 6/site/month)
- Start without ads; AI budget USD 200–300 for first 10 clients

## Open items
- Test price in Argentina; fall back to USD 79 there if resistance (keep ~USD 99 in MX, CL, UY, etc.)
- 30-day test: 1 niche + 1 city, 100 businesses, success = 5+ paying
- Automate edits, renewals, domains and WhatsApp support before scaling
- Feature idea: generate site from Instagram / Google Maps link
